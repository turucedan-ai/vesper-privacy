# Vesper Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Vesper is a game for Android, published on Google Play. This policy covers the Android app.

## What stays on your phone
Your scores, settings, ship choice, and progress are saved on your phone only. We don't send them to us, and we don't have accounts. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

## Ads
The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, an approximate location based on your IP address, how you interact with ads, and crash and diagnostic information. Google handles that data under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. In regions that require it, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's Android settings.

## Remove Ads purchase
Remove Ads is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether you own the purchase, so it can turn ads off and restore the purchase on a new phone.

## What we don't do
We don't sell your data. We don't collect your name, email, contacts, photos, or precise location.

## Children
Vesper is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
